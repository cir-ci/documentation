## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Create a project.** Use the dashboard.

2. **Copy its token.** Store it in your CI secrets as <code>CIRCI_TOKEN</code>.

3. **Run the upload.**

   <CopyCommand client:visible command="npx circi upload ./screenshots --wait" />

   <Aside type="note">An Aside indented under its step stays inside the step: use it for a warning that belongs to that one action. Anything longer than a short paragraph ends the list: close <code>&lt;/Steps&gt;</code>, write the text, and open a new <code>&lt;Steps&gt;</code> that continues the numbering with the <code>start</code> attribute of the list.</Aside>

4. **Open the build.**

</Steps>

## Callouts

<Aside type="note">A note is information, in the processing color.</Aside>
<Aside type="tip">A tip is something that went right, in the approved color.</Aside>
<Aside type="caution">A caution needs a decision, in the needs review color.</Aside>
<Aside type="danger">A danger breaks something, in the failed color.</Aside>

## Keys and settings

Press <Kbd keys="Shift+A" /> to approve everything, or <Kbd keys="Mod+K" /> for the command menu. A sequence uses "then": <Kbd keys="g then i" /> opens the Inbox.

<SettingCard name="PUBLIC_URL" defaultValue="http://localhost:3000" type="URL">
The address people open in the browser. It's also the base of every link CirCI sends.
</SettingCard>

<SettingCard name="BETTER_AUTH_SECRET" required>
Signs sessions. 32 characters or more.
</SettingCard>
