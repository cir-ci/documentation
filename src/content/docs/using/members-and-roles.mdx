People belong to a workspace with one of three roles, and are added to the projects they work on. The role decides what someone can change; project membership decides what they can see.

## Roles

| | Owner | Admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|
| See the workspace, its Members and projects lists | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| See every project | Yes | Yes | Only projects they belong to |
| Approve, reject, comment and ask for reviews on a project they can see | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Change a project's settings, rules, webhooks and token; create projects | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add anyone to a project, remove anyone from it | Yes | Yes | Leave a project themselves |
| Change workspace settings, integrations, sign-in and storage | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite and remove people, change roles | Yes | Yes | No |
| Delete anyone's comment | Yes | Yes | Own comments only |
| Delete the workspace | Yes | No | No |

A project that someone can't see doesn't exist for them: they get "not found" rather than "forbidden", so the existence of a project isn't revealed. Only the author of a comment edits it or moves its region, whatever their role.

Separately from these, an **instance admin** runs the whole installation: accounts, all workspaces, health and instance settings. It isn't a workspace role. See [Instance administration](/using/instance-administration/).

## The Members page

Open it from **Settings** in the workspace menu, then **Members**. It is the one workspace settings page every member can open. Each person is a row with their name and email, tags for how they sign in (for example a password or a connected account), the projects they're in, and their role. The header counts people, and the find field filters by name, email or role.

Owners and admins also see:

- **Invite** in the page header.
- A role control on every row except their own, and an actions menu with **Remove from workspace**.

### Invite someone

<Steps>

1. Choose **Invite**.

2. Enter the **Email** and a **Role**: Admin or Member. Owners aren't invited; a workspace's first owner is whoever created it.

3. Choose **Send invite**. The toast says "Invitation sent to …".

</Steps>

An invitation is valid for 7 days. Inviting an address that already has a pending invitation replaces it: the earlier link stops working, and the toast says so. The people you've invited but who haven't accepted yet are under **Pending invitations**, each with its role and when it expires. **Resend** sends it again, and **Revoke** cancels it.

Invitations need mail: see [Instance administration](/using/instance-administration/). Without an invitation, people can also join through a verified email domain or the workspace's single sign-on; see [Workspace settings](/using/workspace-settings/).

### Change a role

Choose Admin or Member in the person's role control. A dialog confirms first ("Make Ann an admin?"):

- **To admin**: they can invite and remove people, change roles and change every project's settings.
- **To member**: they can no longer invite or remove people, or change settings.

The owner's role isn't changed here, and nobody changes their own.

### Remove someone

**Remove from workspace** asks first: they lose access to the workspace and its projects, and you can invite them again later. A removed person's earlier comments and decisions stay in the builds, shown under their name or as "someone who has left".

## People in a project

Being in the workspace isn't enough to see a project. A member sees a project only when they're added to it. Owners and admins see all of them, and add or remove members in **Project settings, Members**. The roles shown there are the workspace roles. A project member can **Leave project** themselves. See [Project settings](/using/project-settings/).

Project membership also decides who can be asked to review: the request list, review rules and triage rotation draw only from a project's members. See [Review requests and rules](/using/review-requests-and-rules/).

<Aside type="tip">
For a small team, add everyone to every project. For a larger one, keep each project's membership to the people who review it, so request lists stay short.
</Aside>
