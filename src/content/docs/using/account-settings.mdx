Account settings are about you, not a workspace: they follow you across workspaces and projects. Settings is a closed area of the dashboard, so these pages have their own way back to the app.

## Where to open them

The **account menu** at the bottom of the sidebar (your name) has:

- **Profile and sessions**
- **Notifications**
- a **Theme** control
- **Keyboard shortcuts** (not shown on a phone)
- **Install app**, when your browser can install the dashboard and you haven't yet
- **Instance admin**, for instance admins only
- **Sign out**

<Kbd keys="Mod+K" /> also opens any of these pages by name, including **Appearance**. Inside settings, the sidebar lists the account pages (Profile, Notifications, Appearance, Keyboard shortcuts) above the instance, workspaces and projects.

## Profile

### Details

- **Display name** saves when you confirm it with its check mark ("Name saved").
- **Email**. With mail set up on the server, changing it sends a confirmation to the new address ("Check … to confirm the change"), and the line "Waiting for confirmation of …" shows until you click the link. Without mail, the field is read-only: only an instance admin can change your email.
- The picture isn't uploaded. CirCI uses the picture of the account you sign in with, then Gravatar for your email, then a generated avatar.

### Password

Shown only when your account has a password. A single sign-on-only account has none. **Change password** asks for the current password, a new one and a confirmation. A password is at least 12 characters, and changing it keeps this device signed in.

### Signed in with

The accounts you can sign in with, each marked Linked or Not linked. **Link** connects a provider the instance offers, and **Remove** disconnects one (after a confirmation). You can't remove your only way to sign in. Linking GitHub also lets you approve and reject from GitHub checks and pull request comments, as yourself. See [GitHub](/integrations/github/).

### Sessions

Every browser signed in as you, with a description such as "Firefox on macOS", its address and when it signed in. **Sign out** ends one; **Sign out everywhere else** ends all but this device, after a confirmation. Ending a session also cuts off its access to [hosted sites](/using/hosted-sites/) within a minute.

## Notifications

Everything that needs you lands in the [Inbox](/using/inbox-and-reviews/), and none of it can be switched off; you snooze or delete single items instead. This page chooses what also reaches you outside the dashboard. Switches save as soon as you flip them, with a toast ("Review requested on, saved").

### Email

Only available when the server can send mail. Two switches, mailed to the address shown on the page:

| Switch | Sends |
|---|---|
| Review requested | An email when someone asks you to review a build |
| Review overdue | An email when a review you owe has waited past its target |

### Push

Browser or phone notifications for this device, a switch per device. **This device** shows first, your other devices follow, each with **Remove**. Below that, choose which kinds to push; the choice applies to every device that is on:

| Kind | Pushes |
|---|---|
| Review requested | Review requests, including again after a push, and overdue reviews |
| Mentioned | Someone @mentions you in a comment |
| Rejected or approved | A build you pushed was reviewed. Rejections include the reason |

Where the browser can't push, the device row says why and what to do. The browser's own permission box only appears after you turn notifications on; if you decline, the browser won't ask again and you change it in its site settings. On an iPhone, notifications work only from the Home Screen, and the page walks you through adding CirCI there. The Reviews page and the Inbox also offer to turn notifications on after a review lands or after your first approval; "Not now" asks again after 14 days, at most three times.

### What lands in the Inbox

The page lists the kinds, so you know what to expect: review requested, mentioned, your build was approved or rejected, overdue review, connection needs attention (owners and admins only), and flaky screenshot detected (to the week's triage owner). Slack channels for a project are set by an admin in workspace integrations, not here. See [Slack](/integrations/slack/).

## Appearance

**Theme**: System (follows your computer), Light or Dark, each shown as a small preview. It is kept in this browser only, so another browser starts with its own choice. Screenshots under review always show on white. The account menu's Theme control, and <Kbd keys="Mod+K" /> then "theme", change it from anywhere. The change applies at once and needs no Save.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Every shortcut in one page, grouped as in the `?` dialog: General, Navigation, Tabs, Lists, Inbox, Build review and Triage. Press <Kbd keys="?" /> anywhere to see them. The main ones:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <Kbd keys="Mod+K" /> | Open the command menu |
| <Kbd keys="?" /> | Show the shortcut list |
| <Kbd keys="Mod+\" /> | Hide or show the sidebar |
| <Kbd keys="Mod+Shift+C" /> | Copy the link to this page |
| <Kbd keys="g then i" />, <Kbd keys="g then r" />, <Kbd keys="g then p" />, <Kbd keys="g then t" />, <Kbd keys="g then s" /> | Open Inbox, Reviews, projects, Triage, Settings |
| <Kbd keys="j" /> / <Kbd keys="k" />, <Kbd keys="Enter" />, <Kbd keys="Space" />, <Kbd keys="x" />, <Kbd keys="f" /> | Move, open, peek, select, filter in a list |

See [Reviewing a build](/using/reviewing-a-build/), [Inbox and reviews](/using/inbox-and-reviews/) and [Triage and flaky screenshots](/using/triage-and-flaky-screenshots/) for the keys of each screen.

## Install as an app

The dashboard can be installed as an app, with its own window and tabs. An offer appears in the Inbox after your second visit or first approval ("Not now" hides it for 14 days, at most three times), and **Install app** in the account menu is always there while it isn't installed. In the installed app there are tab keys: `Alt` + `t` new tab, `Alt` + `w` close, `Alt` + `1` to `9` go to a tab, `Alt` + `p` pin.
