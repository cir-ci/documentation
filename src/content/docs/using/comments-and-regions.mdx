Every build has a discussion in the **Comments** tab beside the viewer. A comment can be general, or pinned to a rectangle on one screenshot so that everyone looks at the same pixels. Anyone who can see the project can read and write comments.

## Write a comment

Press `c` on the build screen, or open the **Comments** tab and click the field. Type, then press the **Comment** button or `Cmd` + `Enter`. A comment is at most 5000 characters. The tab's badge counts the comments in the build.

- **Reply** under a comment starts a reply. Threads are one level deep: a reply can't have replies of its own, so reply to the first comment of the thread.
- **Edit** and **Delete** show under your own comments. An edited comment says "(edited)". Owners and admins of the workspace can delete anyone's comment, but only the author edits it.
- Deleting asks first ("It can't be restored") and leaves "This comment was deleted." in the thread.

### Mention people

Type `@` and pick a person from the list. A mention notifies that person in their Inbox, and by push when they have it on. See [Inbox and reviews](/using/inbox-and-reviews/) and [Account settings](/using/account-settings/).

A mention is matched to a project member by the part of their email before the `@`, or by their name written as one word (`annlee`, `ann-lee`, `ann.lee`, `ann_lee`), ignoring case. If a handle fits nobody, or fits two members, it mentions no one and nobody is notified. Pick from the list to be sure. When you edit a comment, only people you newly mention are notified.

## Pin a comment to a region

1. Open the screenshot in any compare mode.
2. Drag a rectangle over the part you mean, on the baseline or on the new screenshot.
3. Write the comment in the box that opens. `Cmd` + `Enter` sends it, **Cancel** or `Esc` drops it.

The region appears as a numbered marker on the image. The same number shows beside the thread in the Comments tab, and the thread's chip names the screenshot. Click a marker to jump to its thread, or the chip to jump to its screenshot. A region remembers which side it was drawn on, baseline or new.

Regions are fractions of the screenshot, so they stay in place however the image is zoomed. The smallest region is 1% of the screenshot in each direction.

To change a region, drag its corner handles or move it. Only the author does this, and a "Region saved" toast confirms it.

For keyboard users, the **Mark a region with the keyboard** button (the crosshair in the viewer toolbar) starts a region: the arrow keys move it, `Shift` + arrow keys resize it, `Enter` confirms, `Esc` cancels.

<Aside type="note">
Drawing is offered only when the screenshot has a new image, so a removed screenshot can't get one. A reply never has a region of its own.
</Aside>

## Dragging can also draw ignore regions

Owners and admins get a second choice in **View options** under **Dragging draws**: **Comments** or **Ignore regions**. With Ignore regions, a drag adds an area to leave out of every future comparison of that screenshot. This is a different thing from a comment region: it changes how builds are judged. See [Baselines and ignore regions](/using/baselines-and-ignore-regions/). Members only see the Comments behaviour.

## Where comments show up

- The **Activity** tab lists each comment ("commented on checkout/cart.png"); selecting it opens the thread.
- A mention in the Inbox opens the build on the Comments tab, with the screenshot and thread selected.
