Every build has a discussion in the **Comments** tab beside the image. A comment can be general, or pinned to a rectangle on one screenshot. Anyone who can see the project can read and write comments.

## Write a comment

Press <Kbd keys="c" /> on the build page, or open the **Comments** tab and click the field. Type, then press the **Comment** button or <Kbd keys="Mod+Enter" />. A comment is at most 5000 characters. The tab's badge counts the comments in the build.

- **Reply** under a comment starts a reply. A reply can't have replies, so reply to the first comment of the thread.
- **Edit** and **Delete** show under your own comments. An edited comment says "(edited)". Owners and admins of the workspace can delete anyone's comment, but only the author edits it.
- Deleting asks first ("It can't be restored") and leaves "This comment was deleted." in the thread.

### Mention people

Type `@` and pick a person from the list. A mention notifies that person in their Inbox, and by push notification when they have it on. See [Inbox and reviews](/review/inbox-and-reviews/) and [Account settings](/settings/account-settings/).

A mention is matched to a project member by the part of their email before the `@`, or by their name written as one word (`annlee`, `ann-lee`, `ann.lee`, `ann_lee`), ignoring case. If a handle fits nobody, or fits two members, it mentions no one and nobody is notified. Pick from the list to be sure. When you edit a comment, only people you newly mention are notified.

## Pin a comment to a region

<Steps>

1. **Open the screenshot.** Any compare mode works.

2. **Drag a rectangle.** Cover the part you mean, on the baseline or on the new screenshot.

3. **Write the comment.** Use the box that opens. <Kbd keys="Mod+Enter" /> sends it, **Cancel** or <Kbd keys="Esc" /> drops it.

</Steps>

The region appears as a numbered marker on the image. Each comment region has its own color, chosen by its number (the ninth starts the palette again, and the number tells the two apart): the box on the image, its pin and the comment's chip are the same color. The box is outlined so it stands out on any screenshot. The same number shows beside the thread in the Comments tab, in a chip with the screenshot's name ("1 · pricing"), and the screenshot's row in the list shows how many comments it has. Click a marker to jump to its thread, or the chip to jump to its screenshot. A region remembers which side it was drawn on, baseline or new.

Regions scale with the image, so they stay in place at any zoom. The smallest region is 1% of the screenshot in each direction.

To change a region, drag its corner handles or move it. Only the author does this, and a "Region saved" toast confirms it.

For keyboard users, the **Mark a region with the keyboard** button (the crosshair in the toolbar above the image) starts a region: the arrow keys move it, <Kbd keys="Shift" /> + arrow keys resize it, <Kbd keys="Enter" /> confirms, <Kbd keys="Esc" /> cancels.

<Aside type="note">
Drawing is offered only when the screenshot has a new image, so a removed screenshot can't get one. A reply never has a region of its own.
</Aside>

## Ignore regions

Owners and admins get a second choice in the **View options** menu (the ⋯ button above the image) under **Dragging draws**: **Comments** or **Ignore regions**. With Ignore regions, a drag adds an area to leave out of every future comparison of that screenshot. This is a different thing from a comment region: it changes how builds are judged, and it looks different. An ignore region is a hatched box in neutral ink with a numbered tag, with no color, so it can't be taken for a comment. See [Baselines and ignore regions](/review/baselines-and-ignore-regions/). Members only see the Comments behavior.

## Where comments show up

- The **Activity** tab lists each comment ("commented on checkout/cart.png"); selecting it opens the thread.
- A mention in the Inbox opens the build on the Comments tab, with the screenshot and thread selected.
